Fitrah · فطرة

04

The Fitrah path is one level: a single chamber of light whose atmosphere changes with each chapter. Chapter one, Who created me: the player traces a chain of what came before them and discovers that it cannot explain itself; then 52:35 and the answer, Allah, with 39:62. Chapter two, Why am I here: worship, with 51:56, and 6:162 cited in Dalil's explanation. Chapter three, How should I live: the five pillars, met one at a time, from the hadith that Islam is built on five, each pillar with its verse: 47:19, 4:103, 9:103, 2:183 and 3:97. Chapter four, What comes after death: the Hereafter, with 3:185, and Dalil's reminder of mercy from 39:53. The ending gathers the four answers under 30:30, the verse that gives the product its name. Before it starts, the player chooses how familiar Islam is to them, new, the basics, or Muslim, and Dalil words everything for that depth. That choice is not stored with the player.
