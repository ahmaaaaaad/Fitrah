The Water

Drought, rain, a stream, a meadow, and the light. The player is a witness: every gesture follows what the world is already doing. It ends on 57:17.

This is the first Tafakor world, The Water, at its climax, as the game renders it. The player arrives in a cracked, dry valley. Wind gathers the clouds, rain falls, the first water finds its way down the stream bed, a meadow answers, and finally the cloud thins and the light comes through. The player never commands the weather. They are a witness: every gesture follows something the world is already doing, and changes only where they look. The scene ends on Surah Al-Hadid, verse 17: Know that Allah gives life to the earth after its lifelessness; We have made clear to you the signs, perhaps you will understand.
