Every verse gets its moment. The Arabic appears first, word by word in reading order, from an integrity-checked record, in the KFGQPC Uthmanic script, with its reference. Then the translation of the meaning, Saheeh International, labelled as a translation of meaning, never as the Qur'an itself. Arabic readers see the translation only if they ask for it. Then Dalil's explanation, clearly marked as his and not Qur'anic text, worded for the depth the player chose, with Simpler and More detail beside it. The player decides when to continue. Nothing is hurried, and nothing blurs the line between revelation and explanation.
