Fitrah · فطرة

03

From one menu, the player chooses one of two paths. Fitrah, the four questions, is a guided journey through a single chamber of light: four questions, four answers, each answer from its sources. Tafakor, from the Qur'anic call to reflect, is a set of experiential worlds of contemplation on the signs in creation. The first, The Water, is fully playable today; The Mountains and The Space are already listed and come next. Both paths have Dalil as their guide and the same checks on sacred text; the referral to a person and the choice of depth are part of Fitrah for now.
