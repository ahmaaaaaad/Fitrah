11

This is how Dalil answers a free question. First, the question carries its context: which chapter the player is in, the verse on screen, and the last few exchanges. Second, in Fitrah, before any model is involved, the question is checked: questions about rulings and signs of distress go to a person instead. Dalil refers the player and offers Talk to someone; when there are signs of distress, emergency guidance opens straight away. The model is also instructed never to give rulings. Third, other questions go to the model through our own server, so the provider key never reaches the browser; the prompt lists only the approved sources for that level, and the server is rate-limited and does not log questions. Fourth, the whole answer is validated before the player sees a single word: every citation must be an approved key, there may be no Qur'anic marks or vocalised Arabic, no run of three consecutive Arabic words from any stored verse, no wording that credits the player with creating, and enough confidence. If one check fails, the answer is withheld, never repaired, and the player gets the closest reviewed answer, or an honest line that there is no reliable source for it. Without a key or a network, Dalil answers from that reviewed content.
