Fitrah

An interactive journey through the four questions every person asks, answered from Islam’s sources, with a guide beside you.

Assalamu alaikum. This is Fitrah, an interactive experience built for the AI in the Service of Islamic Content Challenge, in the interactive experiences track. What you see is the first frame of the product, exactly as it renders: a small human figure and a guide, standing before an immense circular opening onto a turning spiral of light. Fitrah takes the four questions every person asks and lets the player discover Islam's answers to them, guided, and always from the sources.
