Fitrah · فطرة

02

Every person, at some point, asks four questions. Who created me? Why am I here? How should I live? What comes after death? Most people meet Islam's answers to them as text to read or a lecture to watch. Fitrah turns them into an experience: the player walks through each question; in chapters one and three they do something with their own hands that leads them toward the answer; and in every chapter they read the verse that answers it, with a guide beside them who explains. The answers come from the Qur'an and the Sunnah, never from the model.
