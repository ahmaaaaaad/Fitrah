This is chapter one, and it shows the principle behind every interaction: discovery comes before the answer. The player presses and holds their own small light and drags along a path of light to what came before them: their parents, then those before them, the earth, the sun and the stars. Each link lights up and is named: has a Creator. The last link reaches into the dark and finds nothing there that explains itself. Only then does the verse appear, word by word: Or were they created by nothing, or were they the creators of themselves? Surah At-Tur, verse 35. The instruction is worded for the device in hand: click and hold with a mouse, touch and hold on a phone, and if the player hesitates, Dalil says it another way.
