Fitrah · فطرة

09

The Water is built on five witness gestures. Trace the wind: follow a current that is already moving across the valley. Look through the rain: a lens of attention clears the rain to show the soil drinking. Follow the first water as it runs down the stream bed by itself. Connect what is already related: from the cloud, to the soil, to the water, to the flower, with the next point always clear. And stay with the thinning cloud as the light opens. The player never commands the weather; the world keeps its own course and waits for their attention. That is the principle behind every gesture: the player witnesses the signs; they do not create them. Each instruction names the exact action, for example: touch and hold the glowing point on the cloud, then drag along the path to the next point.
