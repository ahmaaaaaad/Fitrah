Fitrah · فطرة

10

Dalil, whose name means guide, is the AI companion, and he is present from the first frame: a hooded figure carrying his own light, who walks beside the player, turns to them when he speaks and points his light at what he explains. He introduces each question, explains each source at the depth the player chose, and words every instruction for the way they are touching the screen. At any moment the player can ask him anything: tap him, or press Ask Dalil. Here, in chapter three, the player asked what the five pillars are, and his answer cites the five verses behind them. And he is honest about what he is. Under every answer the player reads: Dalil is a guide who explains from approved sources, not a scholar or a mufti.
