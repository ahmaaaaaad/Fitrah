Chapter 3 · How should I live?

Five pillars, one building

Met one by one: its symbol, Dalil’s explanation at the player’s depth, its verse a tap away

Then an arc joins their tops and a band their bases, from the hadith that Islam is built on five

Fitrah · فطرة

Chapter three asks how should I live, and answers with Arkan al-Islam. Five columns of light stand in one arc, in reading order. The player taps a pillar, or Next pillar: it lights, a small symbol of its own appears, one light above the Shahadah, five rings on Salah, light given outward for Zakah, a crescent above Sawm, light circling the base for Hajj. Dalil explains it at the player's depth, and its verse is one tap away. They are symbols, never depictions. When all five have been met, an arc of light joins their tops and a band joins their bases: five pillars, one building. Then comes the hadith reference, that Islam is built on five, and the answer.
