Fitrah · فطرة

16

Where Fitrah goes next. Chapters two and four have their story, sources and answers today, with a guided step; their own interactions are designed and come next. Tafakor grows with The Mountains and The Space, built on the same engine and the same safeguards. Recitation joins once we have secured the licence for one reciter for the whole experience. Talk to someone gets a verified partner behind it, still asking the player's consent before anything is shared. Our draft 50-question test set for Dalil, written for an earlier version, is adapted to Fitrah and reviewed by a specialist, together with the hadith numbering, and then we publish the scores. And because most of what the player reads is already kept out of the rendering code, adding languages is mostly a matter of translation and review.
