Fitrah · فطرة

13

Fitrah is the same experience on a computer and on a phone. Every shot is composed once and adapted to the screen in hand, so a phone held upright sees the same scene rather than a cropped, zoomed-in middle. The quality is matched to the device's graphics chip, and the resolution adjusts to hold the frame rate. So far this has been tested on emulated phones; physical phones are the next check. On a touch screen, touch, hold and drag belong to the game, so no text-selection bar appears in the middle of a gesture. And the whole product works in Arabic, right to left, and in English, left to right, with every instruction worded for the mouse, the finger or the pen.
