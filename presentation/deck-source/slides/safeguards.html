These are the safeguards that run through the whole product. Verse records: the Arabic is the Uthmani text from the Noble Qur'an Encyclopedia and the translation is Saheeh International, both generated from pinned packages, never typed by hand; each ayah carries a SHA-256 fingerprint that the game checks again before showing it. Every piece of text says what it is, so the Qur'an, a translation of its meaning, a hadith and Dalil's own explanation can never be mistaken for one another. Qur'anic wording appears only from those records: our automated check compares every authored line with every stored verse, and it actually caught five Arabic lines written for Dalil that echoed Qur'anic wording; they were rewritten. The hadith of the five pillars is cited by reference, with its text left out until it is entered from a verified edition and its numbering marked to be confirmed. Questions about rulings, and signs of distress, go to a person before any model is asked. And Talk to someone is honest: today it says plainly that no live partner is connected, offers general pathways such as a local mosque, and asks consent before anything would be shared. Nothing the player types is stored; the optional analytics count events, never words or identities.
