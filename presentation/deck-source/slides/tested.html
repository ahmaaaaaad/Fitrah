How do we know it works? First, 698 automated checks run over the Fitrah level's content: the fingerprints of its 16 verses, the status of the hadith reference, all 143 authored lines in Arabic and English, and the instruction wording for mouse, touch and pen; one of these checks is the one that caught five Arabic lines echoing Qur'anic wording. Second, 13 validation cases for The Water's Dalil: good answers pass, while answers that quote a verse or are vocalised like scripture, cite a source that is not approved, label themselves as Qur'an, have low confidence, or give the player agency are withheld; every authored line and the ten device-worded instructions are checked too. Third, the product is played automatically from the menu to the end with real mouse and touch events: Fitrah on a desktop, on an emulated phone held upright and on an emulated phone held sideways in Arabic; The Water on a desktop and an upright phone; all with no console errors. Testing on physical phones is the next step, and so is a Dalil test set adapted to Fitrah.
